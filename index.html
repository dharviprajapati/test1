Featured case study / 01
Human Behaviour & Strategies · 5 weeks

Can a space nudge us toward more mindful behaviour?

How might we activate non-engaged spaces in a temple to encourage meaningful visitor engagement and enhance the overall temple experience?

This project studies temple precincts as socio-spiritual spaces. Through slow ethnography, behavioural categorisation and spatial mapping, the team explored how people interact with the environment — and where design could encourage more mindful participation without aggressive policing.

Research12 temples · Gandhinagar & Ahmedabad
Observed186 behaviours
MethodsFly on the wall, shadowing, body storming
TeamDharvi Prajapati & Vysali Chadalavada
Open full project PDF ↗
01 / OBSERVE

Notice behaviour

Visited 12 temples and documented 186 behaviours using observation logs, photographs and spatial mapping.

02 / SYNTHESISE

Find patterns

Grouped devotional, social, spatial, sensory and transactional behaviours; compared engaged and non-engaged areas.

03 / DEFINE

Frame the opportunity

Investigated root causes behind disengagement and selected a problem area with temple authorities.

04 / INTERVENE

Design for change

Explored heritage storytelling, light and water, projection, responsive steps, reflective seating and community stewardship.
